A few useful details

Before we begin.

Every project starts with a conversation about fit, scope, timelines and the outcome you need. Reach out to explore whether we’re a good match.

What kinds of projects do you take on?

Business websites, landing pages, dashboards, internal tools and suitable custom web solutions. Final fit depends on the requirements, timeline and available capacity.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes, the studio is open to project conversations with clients in India and internationally. Communication, time zones, payment terms and delivery arrangements are agreed before work begins.

How do estimates and timelines work?

After discovery, the estimate is based on agreed deliverables, complexity, dependencies, testing and feedback rounds. Any assumptions and exclusions are documented before kickoff.

Can you work on an existing website or tool?

Potentially, yes. We’ll first review the current setup, access requirements, code ownership and the specific improvements needed before confirming feasibility.

Are the projects shown here client work?

No. Items shown in the selected concepts section are clearly labelled illustrative concept work, not paid client projects or claims of production deployment.
